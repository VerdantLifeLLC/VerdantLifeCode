# The Doors of Stone — Story Bible (fan continuation)

Unofficial fan-written continuation of *The Kingkiller Chronicle*. Not written, authorized, or endorsed by Patrick Rothfuss. Original prose only; no passages reproduced from the published books.

Target length: 400,000–650,000 words (aim ~440k). Chapters ~4,500 words.
Frame = third person (Kote, Bast, Chronicler). Inner story = Kvothe first person, past tense.

## Hidden mythology (reveals across the book)
- Behind the Doors of Stone sleeps Iax, the Shaper who stole the moon. Selitos and the last namers sealed him after the Creation War. The seal is kept by a bloodline (Loeclos → Lockless → Lackless) and by a key of stone (kept in the Lackless box: "a box with no lid or locks").
- Haliax (Lanre) wants the doors opened: only the Shaper who unmade the world's order can unmake him and give him death.
- Selitos's curse is bound to Lanre's true story. Every true telling strengthens the curse; that is why the Chandrian kill those who sing it true (Kvothe's parents). A false story, widely believed and *written* in Yllish knot-script (a true writing that binds), would loosen the curse.
- Master Ash = **Bredon**. Human, very old, kept alive by bargain with Haliax. He commissions Denna's Lanre song and teaches her Yllish knot-writing. "The beautiful game": win by seeming to lose.
- The Chandrian spared Kvothe as a child because he is Lackless blood with a namer's gift — a living key. "He is needed." (Haliax)
- The Cthaeh's words to Kvothe (about Denna's patron) were a lever to put him at the door.
- Auri is Princess Ariel (Kvothe discovers; never tells). He steals her back from the Sleeping King, a barrow king beneath the University.
- Ambrose Jakis climbs Vintas's succession through deaths Bredon engineers; becomes King Ambrose. Kvothe kills him beneath the Lackless house as Ambrose tries to open the doors with Meluan's blood. Kingkiller.
- Kvothe, trying to save Denna, lets his name touch the door; it cracks. Things come through (scrael, skin dancers — the frame's troubles). He can only close it by ceasing to be the key: Denna knots his true name into a cord of her hair. The door shuts, not fully.
- Kvothe becomes Kote ("disaster"). The thrice-locked roah chest holds three things: the knot of his name, the Lackless box (key of stone), and his lute (his music). Only Kvothe can open it; Kote cannot. Paradox.
- Alveron seizes the crown after Ambrose's death: the **Penitent King**. He stages Kvothe's execution (a dead man with dyed hair); the world believes Kvothe dead.
- Vashet reclaims no sword: she renames Caesura **Folly**, says the Adem will not take back a blade that killed a king outside the Lethani.
- Bast met Kvothe on the moon-roads of the Fae; Kvothe freed him from a Sithe snare near the Cthaeh's clearing. Bast is his student ("Reshi").

## Frame twists (Day Three)
- Bast arranged the soldiers who beat Kote at the end of day two, and nudged Chronicler (via Skarpi) to Newarre.
- Telling a story is a naming. Three days of telling have been re-knitting Kvothe's name. Kote knew and chose it.
- As the name returns, the key returns; Cinder feels it and comes to Newarre.
- Kote cannot open the chest. Chronicler's manuscript — Kvothe's story, written true — is enough of his name to open it. He unknots the cord, becomes Kvothe, drives off Cinder by naming him Ferule, and leaves at dawn with Bast and Chronicler for the Doors of Stone.

- ROAH CHEST made one night in the Fishery (Kilvin had roah planks 11 yrs; no nails). Locks: (1) copper sympathy lock by Wil/Kilvin, keyhole shaped like a closed eye, bound to Kvothe-with-name; (2) stone lock by Fela with the two Eolian foundation stones at its heart; (3) DENNA'S KNOT sunk into the wood between them — opens only if other two open and untied by its twin knot, which Denna keeps ("knots find their way home"). Devi lined lid with glowing green alchemical trap. CONTENTS: Sim's poem about the real Kvothe, the LACKLESS BOX (Bast had carried it from the chamber), his LUTE, the NAME-CORD. Removing the cord = true emptiness; red ring mark on left wrist. Kvothe didn't know Denna after.
- Newarre: waystone at the crossroads (anchor stone that drowns names/magic; Bast hears it as a waterfall; chest silent near it). Regulars: Graham, Old Cob, Jake (wife, son TAM), Carter (horse Nelly), Shep, smith Baxter & apprentice AARON (12 at start), miller & wife, widow Rena. Skarpi's purse = gold from the Penitent King via Stapes (letter: Lark walking, has Netalia's eyes).

Bast's heartbreak over Emberlee (yr 3). Scrael came yr 5 (Carter's horse Nelly); Kote killed 6 with iron pokers; brief return of color.
