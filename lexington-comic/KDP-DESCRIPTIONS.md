## Series description (use on the series page and in "About the series")

**Series name:** Lexington

**Every young man has more power inside him than he knows.**

Lexington is a thirteen-year-old with a brilliant mind, a big heart, and a long list of mistakes. In this illustrated comic series, readers follow him as he grows from a kid who can't remember to shower into a young man of faith, character and courage.

Each issue is a fun, fully illustrated adventure, and each one is built around a real lesson every young man needs: listening to your parents, taking care of yourself, working hard, handling money, telling the truth, serving others, and getting back up when you fall. Lexington doesn't get it right the first time. He fails, he prays, he tries again, and he grows.

And as he grows, something remarkable begins to wake up inside him. In later issues, Lexington discovers inner powers: gifts that grow stronger the more he chooses what's right. The adventures are fictional, but the lessons are real, and they're the ones that shape a boy into a man.

With his loyal family, his big brother Big Tony, his best friend Jonathan, and Biscuit the dog at his side, Lexington is proof that who you become depends on the choices you make, one day at a time.

**Perfect for boys ages 9–14, families, youth groups and anyone raising a young man.**

## Issue #2: Down to Business

**Subtitle:** *A Comic About Money, Hard Work, and Doing Business the Honest Way*

**Three partners. One bucket. A lot to learn.**

Lexington has turned his life around. Now he wants a 3D printer, and he has exactly two dollars and a library card.

So Lexington, his best friend Jonathan, and his big brother Big Tony start the Suds Brothers Car Wash. They borrow eighty dollars from Dad (a loan, not a gift), make a budget, design flyers, and open for business... and nobody shows up. Before the summer is over, the boys will learn what it takes to find customers, what a dollar really costs in hours of hard work, and why honesty is worth more than the fifty-dollar bill they find under a truck seat.

Along the way, they pay their tithing, keep the Sabbath day holy, wash the missionaries' car for free, make up after their first big partner fight, and discover that the best part of earning money is what you learn while earning it.

**Inside:**
• 8 chapters of funny, heartfelt storytelling
• Real money lessons: borrowing and paying back, budgeting, pricing, saving, and the value of hard work
• Honesty, service, keeping the Sabbath, and working as a team
• Scripture from the Bible and the Book of Mormon, including Malachi 3:10, Mosiah 2:17 and Ecclesiastes 4:12
• "The Money Rules": 10 lessons every young entrepreneur should know
• A "Start Your Own Business" worksheet kids can copy and fill in

A great read for Latter-day Saint families, deacons quorums, and any young man ready to earn it.
