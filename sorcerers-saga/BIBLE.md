# THE LATTICE CHRONICLES — Series Bible

Book One: **The Sorcerer's Reckoning**
Book Two: **The Sorcerer's Legacy**
(Series continues: Book Three working title *The Sorcerer's Hollow*.)

Genre: epic fantasy LitRPG / progression fantasy. Third person past tense, mostly Lex's point of view, with Antho chapters (more of them in Book Two) and a few interludes (Elric, Arion, Wren). Rated PG-13: battles and peril, no explicit content, no profanity beyond "damn" / "hells."

Format target: 6 x 9 in KDP paperback, 600–850 pages per book (about 190–210k words each).

---

## 1. The Core Idea

A blacksmith's son who was told he would never be more than a level-capped smith discovers that his father hid a sorcerer's power in his blood, and that the world's **System** (called **the Lattice**) is something his own family built, a cage as much as a gift. His older brother, who everyone believes went mad and murdered their mother, was hit by the same power with no one to guide him. Together they rise in power, find their father, survive a betrayal, learn a magic older than the System, and start to remake the world's rules.

The three big secrets the brothers uncover about their father, **Arion Vey**:
1. (Book 1, early) He was not a merchant who abandoned them. He was a sorcerer of the Order, the **Warden-Architect**, one of the few people alive with **Root** access to the Lattice.
2. (Book 1, Avaloria and the finale) He **sealed their powers** when they were children and left. His seal on Antho failed, and that failure caused Antho's madness and the fire. Worse, he caused the **first breach** that let the Gloam into the world, while trying to rewrite the Lattice to free common people. Ember, the voice in Lex's head, is a fragment of Arion's own mind that he left in the seal.
3. (Book 2) He is **231 years old**, the last direct descendant of **Aurelian Vey**, the ancient sorcerer who wove the Lattice. He has spent two centuries hiding his bloodline from Aurelian's ghost. He also knew all along that their mother, **Seren**, did not die in the fire. She walked into the Hollow to close the door Antho's corrupted power had opened. (Book 2 ends with a message from Seren from inside the Hollow, which is the hook for Book 3.)

## 2. The World: Aldermere

A realm shrouded by mist. The mist is called **the Veil**: ambient mana breathed out by the Lattice. It is thick in valleys and at dawn, and glows faintly silver near Lattice Stones.

**Kingdoms of Aldermere** (loosely united under the Crown of Caldor):
- **Caldor**: the central kingdom. Capital **Highcrest**, seat of King **Aldric III** (old, ailing) and his granddaughter and heir **Princess Lysandra**. Also home to **the Spire**, headquarters of the Wardens of the Lattice.
- **Saltreach**: the coastal free cities to the west. Merchants, smugglers, sea-mages.
- **Vhessa**: the eastern march. Its far edge, now the **Blightmarch**, has been eaten by the Gloam.
- **The Sunreach**: the desert south. Proud, independent and suspicious of the Order. (Mostly Book 2.)

**Key places, Book 1:**
- **Ashbourne**: Lex's village, a small farming and mining village in the southern Vale under the Greyspine. It has a mill, a chapel to the Lady of Lanterns, the Crooked Kettle tavern, Brannoc's forge and a cracked old Lattice Stone on the green.
- **The Greyspine Mountains**: north of Ashbourne. Snow, wyrms, ice trolls, Gloam breaches. **Thistledown** is a trading post on the lower slopes. **The Frostvault** is a small Delve (dungeon). **The Hollowmaw** is the cleft where Antho lives in a cave.
- **The Old Founders' Road**: a ruined paved road north of the Greyspine linking the three **Founders' Shrines**.
- **The Lithic Oracle**: the first shrine, a stone sphinx that asks riddles.
- **The Thornwood**: an enchanted forest that feeds on memory. In its heart is the **Leafbound Archive**, Seraphine's hideout.
- **The Drowned Barrows**: a flooded burial country from the Sundering, full of vengeful spirits and ruled by the **Barrow-King**.
- **Mirrormere**: the Lake of Glass, so still it reflects true selves.
- **The Temple of Avaloria**: on an island in Mirrormere. A Founders' temple and the **Anchor** of the Lattice. Arion's body lies in crystal at its heart.
- **Fort Caldris**: a border fortress where the allies gather, and where the betrayal happens.
- **The Drowned Archive of Qel'Varran**: an ancient library half-sunk in a fen.
- **The Pale Wastes**: a white salt desert. The **Stillwater** is a hidden green valley in it where Malachi lives.
- **Fenmoor**: a Gloam-enslaved village on the Blightmarch border, the first one the brothers free.
- **Duskhold**: Morvane's fortress in the Blightmarch, a black citadel grown from Gloam-stone.

**Key places, Book 2:** the **Forge Academy** (Ashbourne, rebuilt), the **Temple of Ysmir** (a crumbling temple in the north holding the cursed prophecy), the **Glass Desert** (Sunreach), the **Threshold** (where the Hollow touches the world), the four **Wellworlds** (the elemental realms of Flame, Tide, Stone and Gale), the **Umbral Rift** (Elric's fortress), the **Sundered Lands** (beyond the Veil's edge) and **Aurelian's Tomb**, the Heart of the Lattice.

**History:**
- About 900 years ago came **the Sundering**. Sorcerer-kings with unchecked wild magic nearly broke the world. Something old and hungry, the **Unmaker**, woke in the **Hollow**, the empty space behind the world, and began to eat the edges of reality.
- **Aurelian Vey**, greatest of the **Listeners** (the old elemental mages), wove **the Lattice**, an enchantment over the whole world. It rations magic into ordered forms (Classes, Levels, Skills) so no one can wield unchecked power again. It is also the lid on the Hollow. To power the lid, the Lattice secretly takes a **Tithe**, a tenth of all mana everyone uses, and feeds it to the binding. (Revealed in Book 2.) Aurelian anchored his own mind into the Lattice as its **Root**.
- The **Founders**, Aurelian's circle, built the Temples. The **Wardens of the Lattice** were founded to maintain it. Over the centuries the Order turned gatekeeper. It set the **Ceiling** on common classes and sold **Sanctions** (Tier permissions) to the nobility.
- Aurelian, holding the Unmaker for 900 years, has slowly merged with it. His ghost now hunts Vey-blooded bodies to be reborn. (Book 2.)

**Religion and culture:** Most villagers keep the **Lady of Lanterns**, a gentle goddess of hearths, travellers and the lost. People hang lanterns in windows for those who haven't come home. The motif is *a light left on for the lost*. Seren's lullaby, **"The Lantern Song,"** comes from this:

> *Little light, little light, burning low,*
> *keep the door for the ones who go.*
> *If the dark comes calling, don't you mind—*
> *a lantern's lit for the ones behind.*

## 3. THE SYSTEM

The System is a blend of LitRPG traditions:
- a Western **stat/level/class** system (the Lattice);
- **cultivation-style tier breakthroughs** (Tier Trials);
- a **crafting** system (Lex's forging, item grades);
- **use-based skill growth**;
- a hidden **admin/root layer** (Prerogatives and Authority);
- a **sentient companion voice** (Ember) and a hostile one (the Murmur);
- and, from mid-Book 1, an **off-System elemental magic** (Attunement to the Six Wells) that the Lattice can't track or cap.

### 3.1 The Lattice (public layer)

Displayed as **silver-blue** panels only the owner can see. Opened by thought ("Status").

**Kindling:** at sixteen, a youth puts their hand on a **Lattice Stone** and receives a **Class**. The Lattice chooses based on aptitude and blood. Nobles get better classes more often, which is partly the blood and partly Warden rigging.

**Class grades:** Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary, Unique. (Corrupted classes show as **ERROR**.)

**Levels and Tiers:**
| Tier | Levels | Name |
|---|---|---|
| I | 1–24 | Initiate |
| II | 25–49 | Adept |
| III | 50–74 | Master |
| IV | 75–99 | Exalted |
| V | 100 | Sovereign (only Aurelian is known to have reached it) |

To pass from one tier to the next you must complete a **Tier Trial** at a **Trial Gate** (an anvil, an altar or a door at a Founders' site), *and* your Lattice record must carry a **Sanction** flag. Only Root holders and senior Wardens can set it.

**The Ceiling:** Common and Uncommon classes are capped at Level 24 and can never receive a Sanction. "Born to the Ceiling" is a common bitter phrase. Brannoc has sat at 24 for nineteen years.

**Stats (six):**
- **MIG**ht: strength, carrying, melee damage
- **AGI**lity: speed, reflexes, precision
- **VIT**ality: health, endurance, recovery
- **INT**ellect: spell complexity, learning, crafting precision
- **SPI**rit: mana pool, spell power, sensitivity to magic
- **RES**olve: willpower, resistance to fear, charm and corruption, focus under pain

Untrained adult human: about 10 in each. Derived pools: **Health = VIT × 10**, **Mana = SPI × 10**, **Stamina = (MIG + VIT) × 5**.

**Points per level:** Common classes get 3 free points. Rare gets 4, Epic 5, Legendary 6, Unique 6. Classes also add automatic bonuses per level (listed with each class).

**Skills** grow with use, in ranks **Novice → Apprentice → Journeyman → Expert → Master → Grandmaster → Mythic**, with levels 1–10 inside each rank. Skills are granted by the class, learned by practice, or awarded.

**Other features:**
- **Titles** grant small bonuses, e.g. [Unsealed], [Dragon-Friend], [Breaker of Ceilings].
- **Quests** are offered by the Lattice: Main, Side, Hidden and Chain quests.
- **Achievements** are announced privately. Huge deeds trigger a **World Notice** that every Lattice-bound person sees. These are rare, terrifying and politically explosive.
- **Fellowships** are parties of up to eight that share experience. A **Bloodbond** is a special fellowship between blood kin.
- **Delves** are dungeons the Lattice spawns at mana knots: floors, guardians and rewards.

**Item grades** (crafting): Crude, Common (grey), Fine (green), Superior (blue), Masterwork (purple), Legendary (gold), Mythic (red), and **Unknown** (old, pre-Lattice things the System can't name).

**Messages** appear in boxed panels. Typical forms:
```
:::system
LEVEL UP!
Emberwright — Level 5
+6 free points  |  +2 SPI  +1 INT (class)
:::
```

### 3.2 The Root (hidden admin layer)

Displayed in **ember-gold** text that "bleeds through" the silver. It is written in **Wellscript**, the Founders' glyphs, which Lex can read once unsealed.

Only those of **Vey blood** (and a few stolen keys) can touch the Root. Root powers are called **Prerogatives**. They spend **Authority**, a resource from 0 to 100 that regenerates about 1 point per hour, faster near Lattice Stones and Founders' sites.

| Prerogative | Effect | Authority cost (Book 1) |
|---|---|---|
| **True Sight** | See anyone's true status, hidden stats, seals | 1 |
| **Sanction** | Grant Tier permission | 20 |
| **Unbind** | Break someone's Ceiling (permanently lets them grow past 24 and re-rolls their class upward) | 30 at first, later 10 with Attunement |
| **Seal / Lock** | Lock a class or power | 40 |
| **Anchor** | Bind oneself to a Temple Anchor | all of it |
| **Rewrite** | Change the Lattice's laws locally. Forbidden, and costs life | special |

Using Prerogatives can **ping** the Wardens' **Lattice Watch** at the Spire (they see a flare in that region) and can draw the Gloam, which is attracted to Root-light.

### 3.3 Ember (Lex's companion voice)

When Lex's seal cracks, a voice appears: `GUARDIAN CONSTRUCT — ONLINE`. Lex names it **Ember** for the gold glow of its text. It calls Lex "Smith" at first, then "Lex." It is dry, protective and precise, with an old-fashioned courtesy, and it is fond of small terrible jokes about metallurgy. Its memory is locked in sections that open as Lex grows ("Memory partition 3 unlocked"). Lex slowly notices it talks like the father he barely remembers. At Avaloria it is confirmed: **Ember is a fragment of Arion's mind**, pressed into the seal "like a maker's mark." At the end of Book 1, Arion offers to take the fragment back. Ember chooses to stay with Lex, and Arion lets it: "It was always a gift."

In prose Ember speaks in **italic brackets**: *[Good evening, Smith. Please do not die. I have only just arrived.]*

### 3.4 The Murmur (Antho's hostile voice)

Antho's seal broke at his Kindling, and the Lattice gave him a corrupted class. His Root access opened a crack onto the **Hollow**, and the hunger beyond whispers through it. That whisper is the **Murmur**. In prose it appears in **italic brackets with broken text**: *[hungry… little brother smells of warm iron… so hungry…]*

**Clarity** (Antho only, 0–100) measures how much of Antho is in charge.
- It falls when he uses Hollow powers, takes wounds, is afraid or is alone.
- It rises with sleep, music (his bone flute, playing "The Lantern Song"), Lex's presence (Bloodbond passive) and later Shade Attunement.
- **Below 20:** the Murmur can act through him.
- **Below 10:** blackout. He becomes the "mad warlock" of legend.
- At the reunion it is 31. After Malachi's training it stabilises around 70–85.

### 3.5 Attunement: the Six Wells (off-System magic)

Taught by **Malachi** in Book 1, Part Two. Before the Lattice, mages **listened** to the elemental forces directly. The Lattice only *channels* that power, so Attunement is invisible to it. The interface shows it as `UNTRACKED`.

**The Six Wells:** **Flame**, **Stone**, **Tide**, **Gale**, **Radiance** (light and spirit) and **Shade** (void and shadow).

**Stages of Attunement:** **Listening → Answering → Speaking → Commanding → Becoming.**

Practice is cultivation-like: breath work, stillness, emotional honesty, and "letting the Well hear your true name."
- **Lex:** Flame (primary) and Stone. In Book 2 he adds Tide and Gale in the Wellworlds, and touches Radiance at the climax.
- **Antho:** Shade (primary) and Gale. In Book 2 he adds Tide and touches Radiance in his redemption beat. Shade Attunement is what lets him use darkness *without* the Murmur.
- **Wren:** Gale (Listening, Book 1 end; Answering, Book 2).

### 3.6 The combined power: [Reckoning]

This is a **Bloodbond Prerogative** unlocked in Book 1, Chapter 23. Both brothers pour their Authority together, and it *judges* a target's tie to the Lattice and to the Hollow, burning away whatever is unlawful: Gloam, stolen Authority, corrupted grafts. To the eye it is a storm of gold fire and violet shadow braided together. It evolves at Book 1's climax with Attunement (**Reckoning: Dusk and Ember**) and again in Book 2 (**Reckoning: Worldforge**).

## 4. CHARACTERS

### Lex (Lexander Vey), protagonist
- 19 at the start of Book 1 (22 in Book 2). He has used the surname **Harrow**, his mother's, all his life.
- He is the apprentice and adopted son of Brannoc, Ashbourne's smith, and was told at his Kindling three years ago that he was a **[Smith]**, Common, Ceiling 24. That class was a mask, his father's seal.
- **Looks:** tall, broad-shouldered from the forge, burn-scarred forearms, dark curly hair he cuts himself badly, grey eyes that turn **ember-gold** when he uses Root.
- **Personality:** practical, stubborn, kind, funny in a deadpan way, a maker at heart. He thinks with his hands, solves problems like metalwork (heat, pressure, patience) and hates waste and cruelty. **Flaw:** he holds grudges like iron holds heat (especially against Antho and his father), and he believes he must carry everything alone.
- **Arc in Book 1:** from "I am what the Stone said I am" to "I am what I choose to make," and from hating Antho to forgiving him and then forgiving his father.
- **Arc in Book 2:** from savior-who-goes-alone to leader-who-shares-the-load, and from a smith of weapons to a smith of the world's rules.
- **Classes:** [Smith] (masked, Common) → **[Emberwright]** (Unique, Tier I–II: "sorcery through making," runes hammered into metal and air) → **[Forgeheart Sorcerer]** (Tier III, Book 1 finale) → **[Worldforger]** (Tier IV, Book 2 finale, the first since Aurelian).
- **Weapons:** the rune-hammer **Anvilsong** (forged in Book 1, Chapter 3), and later the blade **Lanternbrand** (Book 2).
- **Emberwright class bonus:** +2 SPI and +1 INT per level, plus 6 free points.

### Antho (Anthoren Vey), deuteragonist
- 24 at the start of Book 1, five years older than Lex.
- Kindled at 16 (eight years before Book 1, when Lex was 11). The seal shattered, the Lattice gave him **[Hollow-Touched Warlock]** (grade: ERROR), and the Murmur flooded in. That night the Harrow cottage burned and their mother **Seren** vanished; everyone believed she died in the fire and that Antho killed her. Antho fled into the Greyspine and became the feared "mad warlock of the Hollowmaw."
- **The truth:** he was trying to hold shut a door into darkness that his power had torn open, and he saw Seren walk *into* it and close it from the other side.
- **Looks:** gaunt, long black hair, a beard grown wild, burn scars on his hands, eyes that go fully black when the Murmur rises, and a smile that, rarely, is exactly Lex's.
- **Personality:** brilliant, bitter, theatrical when scared, tender with animals, a scholar of forbidden things. He talks too much or not at all. Guilt is the whole architecture of him.
- **Companion:** **Ashka**, a young storm-drake (a small dragon) with a torn wing that he saved from Warden hunters. She is cat-sized at first and grows to horse-sized by the end of Book 1 and to a true dragon in Book 2. She speaks in images and feelings, and later a few words.
- **Instrument:** a bone flute. He plays "The Lantern Song" to hold the Murmur off.
- **Arc in Book 1:** from monster to brother. He learns he is not his worst night.
- **Arc in Book 2:** he wants to prove he is more than the redeemed one and goes into the forbidden realms alone. He becomes the guardian of the threshold between the world and the Hollow, a sacrifice he chooses.
- **Classes:** **[Hollow-Touched Warlock]** (ERROR) → **[Twilight Warlock]** (Tier III, Book 1 finale) → **[Threshold Warden]** (Tier IV, Book 2).
- **Powers:** shadow-binding, void-bolts, Hollow Gates (short rifts), pacts with spirits and the dead, and later wind (Gale).

### Arion Vey, the father
- Appears to be about 45. He is really 231 (Book 2). Silver at the temples, a quiet voice, a habit of tapping two fingers on any surface (Ember's "tap" sound in Lex's head is the same rhythm).
- He was **Warden-Architect** of the Spire: Tier IV, Level 88, **[Warden-Architect]**, with Root.
- **Why he left:** he discovered the Order's rigging and tried a forbidden Rewrite to break the Ceiling for everyone. It failed and tore the **first breach** (the "Vhessa Breach," 16 years ago), letting the Gloam in. Morvane, investigating it, was infected. Arion sealed his sons' blood to hide them from the Order (who would make them tools), from the Gloam and, secretly, from Aurelian's ghost. Then he went to Avaloria to anchor himself and hold the breaches shut from inside the Lattice.
- In Book 1 he appears as an **ethereal projection** at Avaloria, with his body in crystal. He is captured via Elric's betrayal, held in Duskhold and freed at the finale.
- In Book 1's finale he becomes advisor to Princess, then Queen, Lysandra.
- In Book 2 the secret of his age and of Seren comes out. He is a good man who chose secrecy over trust, again and again.

### Seren Vey (née Harrow), the mother
Gentle, fierce and a hedge-witch (an unlatticed Listener, which is illegal). She sang "The Lantern Song." Believed dead. Actually she walked into the Hollow eight years ago to close the door. She appears only in memories in Book 1. In Book 2: a lullaby in the Threshold, Arion's confession, and the final message.

### Elric Thorne, mentor and traitor
- About 60. **[Stormcaller]**, Tier III, Level 67. He wears a grey cloak sewn with tiny brass storm-charms, carries a lightning-scarred ash staff and keeps a raven named **Quill**. He is charming, avuncular, funny and an excellent teacher.
- Arion's friend for forty years, always second. Arion never aged, always went first, got Root by blood, and refused to Sanction Elric for Tier IV ("You aren't ready, old friend"). Elric's envy is a slow rot he calls loyalty.
- He comes to Lex in Chapter 1 genuinely wanting to save Arion, and also wanting to be the one who does it.
- **Seeds in Book 1:** he flinches when Ember speaks in Arion's cadence; asks too-specific questions about Root; is absent during two attacks; writes letters he burns; plants evidence against Seraphine; is bitter about "blood" versus "work"; and gives the line, "Some of us have to earn what others are born holding."
- **The turn (Chapter 25):** when Arion's projection names the brothers as the only ones who can mend the Anchor, and tells Elric gently he still isn't ready, Elric accepts Morvane's offer. He opens the wards of Fort Caldris, steals Arion's crystal body and its Authority, locks Lex's class and breaks Antho's Clarity.
- He escapes at the Book 1 finale with a shard of Arion's Authority. In Book 2 he wields the **Key of Shadows** and leads the **Eclipse Concord**. In the end he realises Aurelian's ghost used him too, "always someone's second," and dies relocking the Key. His last words are for Arion.

### Wren Ashdown, ally and love interest
- 20. A tracker and guide from Thistledown, **[Tracker]**, Common, Level 19 (Ceiling 24).
- Quick, wry, practical and fearless with heights. She whistles bird calls, carries a short bow and two knives, and has a fox-red braid and freckles. She has been hungry before and hates people who waste food or lives.
- She was Lex's first Unbinding (Chapter 33) and became **[Pathfinder]** (Rare), then **[Mistwalker]** (Epic, Book 2). She has Gale Attunement.
- She falls for Lex before he has power back, which matters to him: she chose him with no class at all.
- **Book 2:** together; angry when he goes alone; Lex proposes at the end with a ring he forged from a nail of the old Ashbourne forge.

### Brannoc Holt, Lex's master
- 58. Ashbourne's smith, **[Smith]**, Level 24 for nineteen years. Huge, bald and soot-bearded, he hums hymns off-key. He took in 11-year-old Lex after the fire. He is the father Lex actually had.
- Lex gives him his hammer-oath in Chapter 3, leaves home, and comes back to free Ashbourne in Chapter 35, where Brannoc is Unbound and becomes **[Runesmith]** (Rare). He runs the Forge Academy in Book 2.

### Sir Garrick Dunmore, the disgraced knight
- 52. **[Knight-Errant]**, Tier II, Level 41. He was stripped of his order for refusing to burn a village of unlatticed folk.
- Grizzled, kind, terrible at cards, fond of proverbs he invents ("A man who sharpens his sword at the battle has already bled"). He has a daughter, **Elowen**, he hasn't seen in years.
- His sword **Oathkeeper** is broken. Lex reforges it in Chapter 17.
- **He dies** holding the breach at Duskhold in Chapter 39 so the brothers can reach Morvane. Elowen carries Oathkeeper in Book 2.

### Seraphine Ilvane, the Warden scholar
- 29. **[Lattice Scribe]**, Rare, Level 38. A Warden archivist who fled after finding records of the Tithe and the rigged Kindlings.
- Precise, awkward, brave in a terrified way, with ink-stained fingers and spectacles. She is the best Wellscript reader alive besides Arion.
- **Red herring** for the traitor in Book 1: Elric frames her, and she is nearly killed proving her innocence at Fort Caldris.
- In Book 2 she is Royal Archivist and has a slow-burn bond with Antho.

### Malachi, the hermit sage
- Ageless, small, brown and wrinkled as a walnut, barefoot, laughing. The Lattice shows him as `??? — NO RECORD`.
- He is **the Last Listener**, one of the elemental mages from before the Lattice. In Book 2 we learn he was Aurelian's younger brother and disagreed with the weaving: "You do not cage a storm by becoming the bars."
- He teaches the Six Wells in Book 1. In Book 2 he returns, confesses and **dies** binding Aurelian's last shard.

### Daxian Roole, the Warden hunter
- 40s. Warden-Captain, **[Inquisitor]**, Tier II, Level 46. Hunts unlatticed sorcery. Relentless and honest, he believes in law.
- He chases Lex through Part One. Lex spares him (Chapter 18). He sees the Order's corruption and Morvane's taint, defects in Chapter 35 and leads the reformed Wardens after Book 1. He is an ally in Book 2.

### Morvane, the Pale Warden (Book 1 villain)
- Formerly Warden-Commander. He was sent to investigate the Vhessa Breach and touched the Gloam, and it filled him.
- He is white-skinned with veins of black mist, and speaks softly and reasonably. He believes order is mercy and that free will is the disease. He calls himself **the Pale Warden** and his Gloam-thralls "the Quiet."
- His goal is to seize Avaloria's Anchor and rewrite the Lattice so that all power flows through him: "One will. No more war."
- He is **[Gloam Sovereign]**, Tier IV, Level 79. His fortress is Duskhold. His great beast is **Sorrowmaw**, a Gloam-corrupted dragon.
- Defeated by the brothers' [Reckoning: Dusk and Ember] in Chapter 39.

### High Warden Isaura Kell
- The head of the Order: cold, political, Tier IV. She enforces the Ceiling as "stability." She opposes Morvane but won't help the "wildcasters."
- She is deposed after Book 1, and in Book 2 joins Elric's Eclipse Concord.

### King Aldric III and Princess Lysandra
- Aldric is old and sick. He dies between Books 1 and 2.
- Lysandra is 23 in Book 1: sharp, reformist and brave. She is crowned Queen and Arion advises her. Book 2: Queen Lysandra.

### Book 2 additions

  - **Kestrel Varn**: fire-witch, sardonic. She wants the Key to restore her brother **Tamsin**'s stolen name.

  - **Ilse and Ivo Marrow**: twin illusionist thieves, mercenary, who become loyal.
  - **Oriel Sant**: an old blind seer.
- **Lord Chancellor Varric Sorn**: a noble conspirator in the Eclipse Concord.
- **Vharuun**: an ancient storm-dragon, Ashka's mother, believed dead. One of the "ancient beings" of the forbidden realms.
- **The Grey Ferryman**: an ancient being of the Threshold who takes payment in memories.
- **The Hollow Choir**: four sorcerers corrupted by Aurelian's ghost (Book 2, Part Two bosses):
  - **The Cantor**, a voice-mage;

  - **Ysolde the Unnamed**.

- **Aurelian Vey**: the ancient sorcerer, Root of the Lattice, merged with the Unmaker. Book 2's final enemy, the **Hollow King**.

## 5. ANTAGONIST FORCES

- **The Gloam**: black, oily mist that seeps from breaches in the Lattice and smells of wet ash. It corrupts land (grey grass, silent birds) and people (the **Gloamed**, or "the Quiet": thralls with blank eyes who whisper in unison).
- **Gloam creatures:** gloamhounds (Book 1, Chapter 2), mistwraiths, hollow-knights (armoured Gloamed), the rock-wyrm of the Greyspine (only partly Gloamed), and **Sorrowmaw** the Gloam-wyrm.
- **The Order of Wardens**: an institution, not evil, but rigid, corrupt at the top and frightened. Its rank and file include good people.
- **Book 2:**
  - the **Unnamed**: people whose names were deleted from the Lattice by the Key's power. They become hollow shells, then Hollow-born.
  - the **Eclipse Concord**: Elric, High Warden Kell (deposed), Lord Chancellor Sorn and rogue nobles.
  - the **Hollow Choir**.
  - **Aurelian / the Hollow King**.

## 6. BOOK ONE — THE SORCERER'S RECKONING (chapter plan)

**Prologue: The Seal.** Fifteen years earlier. Arion, in a thunderstorm, seals four-year-old Lex and nine-year-old Antho with blood-runes while Seren holds the lantern. Antho, half-asleep, asks, "Will it hurt him?" Arion: "Only if it breaks." He leaves. The Lattice notes it: `Seal Applied ×2. Warden-Architect Arion Vey: CONCEALED.`

### PART ONE: THE AWAKENING
1. **The Forge at Ashbourne.** Lex's life: his Level 9 [Smith] status, Brannoc, the Ceiling, the memory of his Kindling, the cracked village Lattice Stone, the village's fear of the "mad warlock" in the mountains (his brother), and Gloam rumours. Ends as a weathered stranger walks into the forge light.

## 7. BOOK TWO — THE SORCERER'S LEGACY (revised plan, written after Book One)

**Setting:** three years after Duskhold, two years after the first Festival. Lex is 22, a FORGEHEART SORCERER around Lv 60, master-teacher at the Ashbourne Forge. Antho is 27, at the Lantern Keep. Arion advises Queen Lysandra. "A Key has been turned" was Elric claiming the first piece (the BOW). He has spent two years quietly building the ECLIPSE CONCORD with fled High Warden Isaura Kell and Lord Chancellor VARRIC SORN (nobles who lost their monopoly to the Unbinding). The Concord wants to "re-Ceiling" the world. Elric wants Root of his own. Aurelian, speaking to Elric as a kind voice, wants the Key turned.

**The Key of Shadows:** forged by Aurelian from his own shadow as the key to the lid between the Lattice and the Hollow, then split into three: the BOW (Sundered Lands, found), the BIT (the Glass Desert, Sunreach) and the BLADE (buried beneath the Hollowmaw; the reason a breach opened there). Even one piece can "unwrite" a person's thread from the Lattice. The victims are the UNNAMED, whom everyone forgets, and their freed mana feeds Elric's Authority. That is how he has grown strong.

**Book Two secrets revealed:**
- Arion is 231. He is the last direct descendant of Aurelian and was once Aurelian's chosen vessel. He hid his sons from Aurelian as well as from the Order.
- The TITHE: the Lattice skims a tenth of all mana to feed the binding, and Aurelian (merged with the Unmaker) eats it.
- The "prophecy of return" was written by Aurelian himself to lure his descendants to his tomb.
- Seren is alive in the Hollow. She has been keeping the door from the other side and holding Aurelian back. Arion has known since the festival year.

## 8. Style rules
- System panels use `:::system` (silver Lattice), `:::root` (gold Root/Ember) and `:::error` (Antho's corrupted panels, the Murmur) blocks. Keep lines at 44 characters or fewer.
- Ember speaks inline as *[italic bracketed]*. The Murmur speaks inline as *[italic bracketed, broken…]*.
- Show numbers when they matter (level-ups, key stats, costs). Don't bury chapters in tables.
- Each chapter should have a hook ending, a real emotional beat and at least one moment of humour.
- Fights read as fights: concrete terrain, cost, clever use of skills, and consequences such as injury and mana exhaustion.
- Lex's magic is *physical*: heat, pressure, hammer-rhythm, the ring of struck metal. Antho's magic is *cold and quiet*: absence, hush, the space between heartbeats.
- Running continuity of levels, stats and skills is kept in `CONTINUITY.md`.
