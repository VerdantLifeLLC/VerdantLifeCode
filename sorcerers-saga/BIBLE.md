# THE LATTICE CHRONICLES — Series Bible

Book One: **The Sorcerer's Reckoning**
Book Two: **The Sorcerer's Legacy**
Book Three: **The Sorcerer's Hollow**
(The series ends with Book Five. See section 9.)

Genre: epic fantasy LitRPG / progression fantasy. Third person past tense, mostly Lex's point of view, with Antho chapters (more of them in Book Two) and a few interludes (Elric, Arion, Wren). Rated PG-13: battles and peril, no explicit content, no profanity beyond "damn" / "hells."

Format target: 6 x 9 in KDP paperback, 600–850 pages per book (about 190–210k words each).

## 1. The Core Idea

A blacksmith's son who was told he would never be more than a level-capped smith discovers that his father hid a sorcerer's power in his blood, and that the world's **System** (called **the Lattice**) is something his own family built, a cage as much as a gift. His older brother, who everyone believes went mad and murdered their mother, was hit by the same power with no one to guide him. Together they rise in power, find their father, survive a betrayal, learn a magic older than the System, and start to remake the world's rules.

The three big secrets the brothers uncover about their father, **Arion Vey**:
1. (Book 1, early) He was not a merchant who abandoned them. He was a sorcerer of the Order, the **Warden-Architect**, one of the few people alive with **Root** access to the Lattice.
2. (Book 1, Avaloria and the finale) He **sealed their powers** when they were children and left. His seal on Antho failed, and that failure caused Antho's madness and the fire. Worse, he caused the **first breach** that let the Gloam into the world, while trying to rewrite the Lattice to free common people. Ember, the voice in Lex's head, is a fragment of Arion's own mind that he left in the seal.
3. (Book 2) He is **231 years old**, the last direct descendant of **Aurelian Vey**, the ancient sorcerer who wove the Lattice. He has spent two centuries hiding his bloodline from Aurelian's ghost. He also knew all along that their mother, **Seren**, did not die in the fire. She walked into the Hollow to close the door Antho's corrupted power had opened. (Book 2 ends with a message from Seren from inside the Hollow, which is the hook for Book 3.)

## 2. The World: Aldermere

A realm shrouded by mist. The mist is called **the Veil**: ambient mana breathed out by the Lattice. It is thick in valleys and at dawn, and glows faintly silver near Lattice Stones.

**Kingdoms of Aldermere** (loosely united under the Crown of Caldor):
- **Caldor**: the central kingdom. Capital **Highcrest**, seat of King **Aldric III** (old, ailing) and his granddaughter and heir **Princess Lysandra**. Also home to **the Spire**, headquarters of the Wardens of the Lattice.
- **Saltreach**: the coastal free cities to the west. Merchants, smugglers, sea-mages.
- **Vhessa**: the eastern march. Its far edge, now the **Blightmarch**, has been eaten by the Gloam.
- **The Sunreach**: the desert south. Proud, independent and suspicious of the Order. (Mostly Book 2.)

**Key places, Book 1:**
- **Ashbourne**: Lex's village, a small farming and mining village in the southern Vale under the Greyspine. It has a mill, a chapel to the Lady of Lanterns, the Crooked Kettle tavern, Brannoc's forge and a cracked old Lattice Stone on the green.
- **The Greyspine Mountains**: north of Ashbourne. Snow, wyrms, ice trolls, Gloam breaches. **Thistledown** is a trading post on the lower slopes. **The Frostvault** is a small Delve (dungeon). **The Hollowmaw** is the cleft where Antho lives in a cave.
- **The Old Founders' Road**: a ruined paved road north of the Greyspine linking the three **Founders' Shrines**.
- **The Lithic Oracle**: the first shrine, a stone sphinx that asks riddles.
- **The Thornwood**: an enchanted forest that feeds on memory. In its heart is the **Leafbound Archive**, Seraphine's hideout.
- **The Drowned Barrows**: a flooded burial country from the Sundering, full of vengeful spirits and ruled by the **Barrow-King**.
- **Mirrormere**: the Lake of Glass, so still it reflects true selves.
- **The Temple of Avaloria**: on an island in Mirrormere. A Founders' temple and the **Anchor** of the Lattice. Arion's body lies in crystal at its heart.
- **Fort Caldris**: a border fortress where the allies gather, and where the betrayal happens.
- **The Drowned Archive of Qel'Varran**: an ancient library half-sunk in a fen.
- **The Pale Wastes**: a white salt desert. The **Stillwater** is a hidden green valley in it where Malachi lives.
- **Fenmoor**: a Gloam-enslaved village on the Blightmarch border, the first one the brothers free.
- **Duskhold**: Morvane's fortress in the Blightmarch, a black citadel grown from Gloam-stone.

**Key places, Book 2:** the **Forge Academy** (Ashbourne, rebuilt), the **Temple of Ysmir** (a crumbling temple in the north holding the cursed prophecy), the **Glass Desert** (Sunreach), the **Threshold** (where the Hollow touches the world), the four **Wellworlds** (the elemental realms of Flame, Tide, Stone and Gale), the **Umbral Rift** (Elric's fortress), the **Sundered Lands** (beyond the Veil's edge) and **Aurelian's Tomb**, the Heart of the Lattice.

**History:**
- About 900 years ago came **the Sundering**. Sorcerer-kings with unchecked wild magic nearly broke the world. Something old and hungry, the **Unmaker**, woke in the **Hollow**, the empty space behind the world, and began to eat the edges of reality.
- **Aurelian Vey**, greatest of the **Listeners** (the old elemental mages), wove **the Lattice**, an enchantment over the whole world. It rations magic into ordered forms (Classes, Levels, Skills) so no one can wield unchecked power again. It is also the lid on the Hollow. To power the lid, the Lattice secretly takes a **Tithe**, a tenth of all mana everyone uses, and feeds it to the binding. (Revealed in Book 2.) Aurelian anchored his own mind into the Lattice as its **Root**.
- The **Founders**, Aurelian's circle, built the Temples. The **Wardens of the Lattice** were founded to maintain it. Over the centuries the Order turned gatekeeper. It set the **Ceiling** on common classes and sold **Sanctions** (Tier permissions) to the nobility.
- Aurelian, holding the Unmaker for 900 years, has slowly merged with it. His ghost now hunts Vey-blooded bodies to be reborn. (Book 2.)

**Religion and culture:** Most villagers keep the **Lady of Lanterns**, a gentle goddess of hearths, travellers and the lost. People hang lanterns in windows for those who haven't come home. The motif is *a light left on for the lost*. Seren's lullaby, **"The Lantern Song,"** comes from this:

> *Little light, little light, burning low,*
> *keep the door for the ones who go.*
> *If the dark comes calling, don't you mind—*
> *a lantern's lit for the ones behind.*

## 3. THE SYSTEM

The System is a blend of LitRPG traditions:
- a Western **stat/level/class** system (the Lattice);
- **cultivation-style tier breakthroughs** (Tier Trials);
- a **crafting** system (Lex's forging, item grades);
- **use-based skill growth**;
- a hidden **admin/root layer** (Prerogatives and Authority);
- a **sentient companion voice** (Ember) and a hostile one (the Murmur);
- and, from mid-Book 1, an **off-System elemental magic** (Attunement to the Six Wells) that the Lattice can't track or cap.

### 3.1 The Lattice (public layer)

Displayed as **silver-blue** panels only the owner can see. Opened by thought ("Status").

**Kindling:** at sixteen, a youth puts their hand on a **Lattice Stone** and receives a **Class**. The Lattice chooses based on aptitude and blood. Nobles get better classes more often, which is partly the blood and partly Warden rigging.

**Class grades:** Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary, Unique. (Corrupted classes show as **ERROR**.)

**Levels and Tiers:**
| Tier | Levels | Name |
|---|---|---|
| I | 1–24 | Initiate |
| II | 25–49 | Adept |
| III | 50–74 | Master |
| IV | 75–99 | Exalted |
| V | 100 | Sovereign (only Aurelian is known to have reached it) |

To pass from one tier to the next you must complete a **Tier Trial** at a **Trial Gate** (an anvil, an altar or a door at a Founders' site), *and* your Lattice record must carry a **Sanction** flag. Only Root holders and senior Wardens can set it.

**The Ceiling:** Common and Uncommon classes are capped at Level 24 and can never receive a Sanction. "Born to the Ceiling" is a common bitter phrase. Brannoc has sat at 24 for nineteen years.

**Stats (six):**
- **MIG**ht: strength, carrying, melee damage
- **AGI**lity: speed, reflexes, precision
- **VIT**ality: health, endurance, recovery
- **INT**ellect: spell complexity, learning, crafting precision
- **SPI**rit: mana pool, spell power, sensitivity to magic
- **RES**olve: willpower, resistance to fear, charm and corruption, focus under pain

Untrained adult human: about 10 in each. Derived pools: **Health = VIT × 10**, **Mana = SPI × 10**, **Stamina = (MIG + VIT) × 5**.

**Points per level:** Common classes get 3 free points. Rare gets 4, Epic 5, Legendary 6, Unique 6. Classes also add automatic bonuses per level (listed with each class).

**Skills** grow with use, in ranks **Novice → Apprentice → Journeyman → Expert → Master → Grandmaster → Mythic**, with levels 1–10 inside each rank. Skills are granted by the class, learned by practice, or awarded.

**Other features:**
- **Titles** grant small bonuses, e.g. [Unsealed], [Dragon-Friend], [Breaker of Ceilings].
- **Quests** are offered by the Lattice: Main, Side, Hidden and Chain quests.
- **Achievements** are announced privately. Huge deeds trigger a **World Notice** that every Lattice-bound person sees. These are rare, terrifying and politically explosive.
- **Fellowships** are parties of up to eight that share experience. A **Bloodbond** is a special fellowship between blood kin.
- **Delves** are dungeons the Lattice spawns at mana knots: floors, guardians and rewards.

**Item grades** (crafting): Crude, Common (grey), Fine (green), Superior (blue), Masterwork (purple), Legendary (gold), Mythic (red), and **Unknown** (old, pre-Lattice things the System can't name).

**Messages** appear in boxed panels. Typical forms:
```
:::system
LEVEL UP!
Emberwright — Level 5
+6 free points  |  +2 SPI  +1 INT (class)
:::
```

### 3.2 The Root (hidden admin layer)

Displayed in **ember-gold** text that "bleeds through" the silver. It is written in **Wellscript**, the Founders' glyphs, which Lex can read once unsealed.

Only those of **Vey blood** (and a few stolen keys) can touch the Root. Root powers are called **Prerogatives**. They spend **Authority**, a resource from 0 to 100 that regenerates about 1 point per hour, faster near Lattice Stones and Founders' sites.

| Prerogative | Effect | Authority cost (Book 1) |
|---|---|---|
| **True Sight** | See anyone's true status, hidden stats, seals | 1 |
| **Sanction** | Grant Tier permission | 20 |
| **Unbind** | Break someone's Ceiling (permanently lets them grow past 24 and re-rolls their class upward) | 30 at first, later 10 with Attunement |
| **Seal / Lock** | Lock a class or power | 40 |
| **Anchor** | Bind oneself to a Temple Anchor | all of it |
| **Rewrite** | Change the Lattice's laws locally. Forbidden, and costs life | special |

Using Prerogatives can **ping** the Wardens' **Lattice Watch** at the Spire (they see a flare in that region) and can draw the Gloam, which is attracted to Root-light.

### 3.3 Ember (Lex's companion voice)

When Lex's seal cracks, a voice appears: `GUARDIAN CONSTRUCT — ONLINE`. Lex names it **Ember** for the gold glow of its text. It calls Lex "Smith" at first, then "Lex." It is dry, protective and precise, with an old-fashioned courtesy, and it is fond of small terrible jokes about metallurgy. Its memory is locked in sections that open as Lex grows ("Memory partition 3 unlocked"). Lex slowly notices it talks like the father he barely remembers. At Avaloria it is confirmed: **Ember is a fragment of Arion's mind**, pressed into the seal "like a maker's mark." At the end of Book 1, Arion offers to take the fragment back. Ember chooses to stay with Lex, and Arion lets it: "It was always a gift."

In prose Ember speaks in **italic brackets**: *[Good evening, Smith. Please do not die. I have only just arrived.]*

### 3.4 The Murmur (Antho's hostile voice)

Antho's seal broke at his Kindling, and the Lattice gave him a corrupted class. His Root access opened a crack onto the **Hollow**, and the hunger beyond whispers through it. That whisper is the **Murmur**. In prose it appears in **italic brackets with broken text**: *[hungry… little brother smells of warm iron… so hungry…]*

**Clarity** (Antho only, 0–100) measures how much of Antho is in charge.
- It falls when he uses Hollow powers, takes wounds, is afraid or is alone.
- It rises with sleep, music (his bone flute, playing "The Lantern Song"), Lex's presence (Bloodbond passive) and later Shade Attunement.
- **Below 20:** the Murmur can act through him.
- **Below 10:** blackout. He becomes the "mad warlock" of legend.
- At the reunion it is 31. After Malachi's training it stabilises around 70–85.

### 3.5 Attunement: the Six Wells (off-System magic)

Taught by **Malachi** in Book 1, Part Two. Before the Lattice, mages **listened** to the elemental forces directly. The Lattice only *channels* that power, so Attunement is invisible to it. The interface shows it as `UNTRACKED`.

**The Six Wells:** **Flame**, **Stone**, **Tide**, **Gale**, **Radiance** (light and spirit) and **Shade** (void and shadow).

**Stages of Attunement:** **Listening → Answering → Speaking → Commanding → Becoming.**

Practice is cultivation-like: breath work, stillness, emotional honesty, and "letting the Well hear your true name."
- **Lex:** Flame (primary) and Stone. In Book 2 he adds Tide and Gale in the Wellworlds, and touches Radiance at the climax.
- **Antho:** Shade (primary) and Gale. In Book 2 he adds Tide and touches Radiance in his redemption beat. Shade Attunement is what lets him use darkness *without* the Murmur.
- **Wren:** Gale (Listening, Book 1 end; Answering, Book 2).

### 3.6 The combined power: [Reckoning]

This is a **Bloodbond Prerogative** unlocked in Book 1, Chapter 23. Both brothers pour their Authority together, and it *judges* a target's tie to the Lattice and to the Hollow, burning away whatever is unlawful: Gloam, stolen Authority, corrupted grafts. To the eye it is a storm of gold fire and violet shadow braided together. It evolves at Book 1's climax with Attunement (**Reckoning: Dusk and Ember**) and again in Book 2 (**Reckoning: Worldforge**).

## 4. CHARACTERS

### Lex (Lexander Vey), protagonist
- 19 at the start of Book 1 (22 in Book 2). He has used the surname **Harrow**, his mother's, all his life.
- He is the apprentice and adopted son of Brannoc, Ashbourne's smith, and was told at his Kindling three years ago that he was a **[Smith]**, Common, Ceiling 24. That class was a mask, his father's seal.
- **Looks:** tall, broad-shouldered from the forge, burn-scarred forearms, dark curly hair he cuts himself badly, grey eyes that turn **ember-gold** when he uses Root.
- **Personality:** practical, stubborn, kind, funny in a deadpan way, a maker at heart. He thinks with his hands, solves problems like metalwork (heat, pressure, patience) and hates waste and cruelty. **Flaw:** he holds grudges like iron holds heat (especially against Antho and his father), and he believes he must carry everything alone.
- **Arc in Book 1:** from "I am what the Stone said I am" to "I am what I choose to make," and from hating Antho to forgiving him and then forgiving his father.
- **Arc in Book 2:** from savior-who-goes-alone to leader-who-shares-the-load, and from a smith of weapons to a smith of the world's rules.
- **Classes:** [Smith] (masked, Common) → **[Emberwright]** (Unique, Tier I–II: "sorcery through making," runes hammered into metal and air) → **[Forgeheart Sorcerer]** (Tier III, Book 1 finale) → **[Worldforger]** (Tier IV, Book 2 finale, the first since Aurelian).
- **Weapons:** the rune-hammer **Anvilsong** (forged in Book 1, Chapter 3), and later the blade **Lanternbrand** (Book 2).
- **Emberwright class bonus:** +2 SPI and +1 INT per level, plus 6 free points.

### Antho (Anthoren Vey), deuteragonist
- 24 at the start of Book 1, five years older than Lex.
- Kindled at 16 (eight years before Book 1, when Lex was 11). The seal shattered, the Lattice gave him **[Hollow-Touched Warlock]** (grade: ERROR), and the Murmur flooded in. That night the Harrow cottage burned and their mother **Seren** vanished; everyone believed she died in the fire and that Antho killed her. Antho fled into the Greyspine and became the feared "mad warlock of the Hollowmaw."
- **The truth:** he was trying to hold shut a door into darkness that his power had torn open, and he saw Seren walk *into* it and close it from the other side.
- **Looks:** gaunt, long black hair, a beard grown wild, burn scars on his hands, eyes that go fully black when the Murmur rises, and a smile that, rarely, is exactly Lex's.
- **Personality:** brilliant, bitter, theatrical when scared, tender with animals, a scholar of forbidden things. He talks too much or not at all. Guilt is the whole architecture of him.
- **Companion:** **Ashka**, a young storm-drake (a small dragon) with a torn wing that he saved from Warden hunters. She is cat-sized at first and grows to horse-sized by the end of Book 1 and to a true dragon in Book 2. She speaks in images and feelings, and later a few words.
- **Instrument:** a bone flute. He plays "The Lantern Song" to hold the Murmur off.
- **Arc in Book 1:** from monster to brother. He learns he is not his worst night.
- **Arc in Book 2:** he wants to prove he is more than the redeemed one and goes into the forbidden realms alone. He becomes the guardian of the threshold between the world and the Hollow, a sacrifice he chooses.
- **Classes:** **[Hollow-Touched Warlock]** (ERROR) → **[Twilight Warlock]** (Tier III, Book 1 finale) → **[Threshold Warden]** (Tier IV, Book 2).
- **Powers:** shadow-binding, void-bolts, Hollow Gates (short rifts), pacts with spirits and the dead, and later wind (Gale).

### Arion Vey, the father
- Appears to be about 45. He is really 231 (Book 2). Silver at the temples, a quiet voice, a habit of tapping two fingers on any surface (Ember's "tap" sound in Lex's head is the same rhythm).
- He was **Warden-Architect** of the Spire: Tier IV, Level 88, **[Warden-Architect]**, with Root.
- **Why he left:** he discovered the Order's rigging and tried a forbidden Rewrite to break the Ceiling for everyone. It failed and tore the **first breach** (the "Vhessa Breach," 16 years ago), letting the Gloam in. Morvane, investigating it, was infected. Arion sealed his sons' blood to hide them from the Order (who would make them tools), from the Gloam and, secretly, from Aurelian's ghost. Then he went to Avaloria to anchor himself and hold the breaches shut from inside the Lattice.
- In Book 1 he appears as an **ethereal projection** at Avaloria, with his body in crystal. He is captured via Elric's betrayal, held in Duskhold and freed at the finale.
- In Book 1's finale he becomes advisor to Princess, then Queen, Lysandra.
- In Book 2 the secret of his age and of Seren comes out. He is a good man who chose secrecy over trust, again and again.

### Seren Vey (née Harrow), the mother
Gentle, fierce and a hedge-witch (an unlatticed Listener, which is illegal). She sang "The Lantern Song." Believed dead. Actually she walked into the Hollow eight years ago to close the door. She appears only in memories in Book 1. In Book 2: a lullaby in the Threshold, Arion's confession, and the final message.

### Elric Thorne, mentor and traitor
- About 60. **[Stormcaller]**, Tier III, Level 67. He wears a grey cloak sewn with tiny brass storm-charms, carries a lightning-scarred ash staff and keeps a raven named **Quill**. He is charming, avuncular, funny and an excellent teacher.
- Arion's friend for forty years, always second. Arion never aged, always went first, got Root by blood, and refused to Sanction Elric for Tier IV ("You aren't ready, old friend"). Elric's envy is a slow rot he calls loyalty.
- He comes to Lex in Chapter 1 genuinely wanting to save Arion, and also wanting to be the one who does it.
- **Seeds in Book 1:** he flinches when Ember speaks in Arion's cadence; asks too-specific questions about Root; is absent during two attacks; writes letters he burns; plants evidence against Seraphine; is bitter about "blood" versus "work"; and gives the line, "Some of us have to earn what others are born holding."
- **The turn (Chapter 25):** when Arion's projection names the brothers as the only ones who can mend the Anchor, and tells Elric gently he still isn't ready, Elric accepts Morvane's offer. He opens the wards of Fort Caldris, steals Arion's crystal body and its Authority, locks Lex's class and breaks Antho's Clarity.
- He escapes at the Book 1 finale with a shard of Arion's Authority. In Book 2 he wields the **Key of Shadows** and leads the **Eclipse Concord**. In the end he realises Aurelian's ghost used him too, "always someone's second," and dies relocking the Key. His last words are for Arion.

### Wren Ashdown, ally and love interest
- 20. A tracker and guide from Thistledown, **[Tracker]**, Common, Level 19 (Ceiling 24).
- Quick, wry, practical and fearless with heights. She whistles bird calls, carries a short bow and two knives, and has a fox-red braid and freckles. She has been hungry before and hates people who waste food or lives.
- She was Lex's first Unbinding (Chapter 33) and became **[Pathfinder]** (Rare), then **[Mistwalker]** (Epic, Book 2). She has Gale Attunement.
- She falls for Lex before he has power back, which matters to him: she chose him with no class at all.
- **Book 2:** together; angry when he goes alone; Lex proposes at the end with a ring he forged from a nail of the old Ashbourne forge.

### Brannoc Holt, Lex's master
- 58. Ashbourne's smith, **[Smith]**, Level 24 for nineteen years. Huge, bald and soot-bearded, he hums hymns off-key. He took in 11-year-old Lex after the fire. He is the father Lex actually had.
- Lex gives him his hammer-oath in Chapter 3, leaves home, and comes back to free Ashbourne in Chapter 35, where Brannoc is Unbound and becomes **[Runesmith]** (Rare). He runs the Forge Academy in Book 2.

### Sir Garrick Dunmore, the disgraced knight
- 52. **[Knight-Errant]**, Tier II, Level 41. He was stripped of his order for refusing to burn a village of unlatticed folk.
- Grizzled, kind, terrible at cards, fond of proverbs he invents ("A man who sharpens his sword at the battle has already bled"). He has a daughter, **Elowen**, he hasn't seen in years.
- His sword **Oathkeeper** is broken. Lex reforges it in Chapter 17.
- **He dies** holding the breach at Duskhold in Chapter 39 so the brothers can reach Morvane. Elowen carries Oathkeeper in Book 2.

### Seraphine Ilvane, the Warden scholar
- 29. **[Lattice Scribe]**, Rare, Level 38. A Warden archivist who fled after finding records of the Tithe and the rigged Kindlings.
- Precise, awkward, brave in a terrified way, with ink-stained fingers and spectacles. She is the best Wellscript reader alive besides Arion.
- **Red herring** for the traitor in Book 1: Elric frames her, and she is nearly killed proving her innocence at Fort Caldris.
- In Book 2 she is Royal Archivist and has a slow-burn bond with Antho.

### Malachi, the hermit sage
- Ageless, small, brown and wrinkled as a walnut, barefoot, laughing. The Lattice shows him as `??? — NO RECORD`.
- He is **the Last Listener**, one of the elemental mages from before the Lattice. In Book 2 we learn he was Aurelian's younger brother and disagreed with the weaving: "You do not cage a storm by becoming the bars."
- He teaches the Six Wells in Book 1. In Book 2 he returns, confesses and **dies** binding Aurelian's last shard.

### Daxian Roole, the Warden hunter
- 40s. Warden-Captain, **[Inquisitor]**, Tier II, Level 46. Hunts unlatticed sorcery. Relentless and honest, he believes in law.
- He chases Lex through Part One. Lex spares him (Chapter 18). He sees the Order's corruption and Morvane's taint, defects in Chapter 35 and leads the reformed Wardens after Book 1. He is an ally in Book 2.

### Morvane, the Pale Warden (Book 1 villain)
- Formerly Warden-Commander. He was sent to investigate the Vhessa Breach and touched the Gloam, and it filled him.
- He is white-skinned with veins of black mist, and speaks softly and reasonably. He believes order is mercy and that free will is the disease. He calls himself **the Pale Warden** and his Gloam-thralls "the Quiet."
- His goal is to seize Avaloria's Anchor and rewrite the Lattice so that all power flows through him: "One will. No more war."
- He is **[Gloam Sovereign]**, Tier IV, Level 79. His fortress is Duskhold. His great beast is **Sorrowmaw**, a Gloam-corrupted dragon.
- Defeated by the brothers' [Reckoning: Dusk and Ember] in Chapter 39.

### High Warden Isaura Kell
- The head of the Order: cold, political, Tier IV. She enforces the Ceiling as "stability." She opposes Morvane but won't help the "wildcasters."
- She is deposed after Book 1, and in Book 2 joins Elric's Eclipse Concord.

### King Aldric III and Princess Lysandra
- Aldric is old and sick. He dies between Books 1 and 2.
- Lysandra is 23 in Book 1: sharp, reformist and brave. She is crowned Queen and Arion advises her. Book 2: Queen Lysandra.

### Book 2 additions

## 5. ANTAGONIST FORCES

- **The Gloam**: black, oily mist that seeps from breaches in the Lattice and smells of wet ash. It corrupts land (grey grass, silent birds) and people (the **Gloamed**, or "the Quiet": thralls with blank eyes who whisper in unison).
- **Gloam creatures:** gloamhounds (Book 1, Chapter 2), mistwraiths, hollow-knights (armoured Gloamed), the rock-wyrm of the Greyspine (only partly Gloamed), and **Sorrowmaw** the Gloam-wyrm.
- **The Order of Wardens**: an institution, not evil, but rigid, corrupt at the top and frightened. Its rank and file include good people.
- **Book 2:**
  - the **Unnamed**: people whose names were deleted from the Lattice by the Key's power. They become hollow shells, then Hollow-born.
  - the **Eclipse Concord**: Elric, High Warden Kell (deposed), Lord Chancellor Sorn and rogue nobles.
  - the **Hollow Choir**.
  - **Aurelian / the Hollow King**.

## 6. BOOK ONE — THE SORCERER'S RECKONING (chapter plan)

**Prologue: The Seal.** Fifteen years earlier. Arion, in a thunderstorm, seals four-year-old Lex and nine-year-old Antho with blood-runes while Seren holds the lantern. Antho, half-asleep, asks, "Will it hurt him?" Arion: "Only if it breaks." He leaves. The Lattice notes it: `Seal Applied ×2. Warden-Architect Arion Vey: CONCEALED.`

### PART ONE: THE AWAKENING
1. **The Forge at Ashbourne.** Lex's life: his Level 9 [Smith] status, Brannoc, the Ceiling, the memory of his Kindling, the cracked village Lattice Stone, the village's fear of the "mad warlock" in the mountains (his brother), and Gloam rumours. Ends as a weathered stranger walks into the forge light.

Books One and Two were about the **father's** secrets. Books Three to Five are about the **mother's**.

### 9.1 The Seventh Well: THE HOLLOW
- The Listeners' Codex (Book One, Ch 27) shows six circles in a ring with an **empty seventh in the centre**. The seventh Well is **the Hollow**: the Well of *room*, the empty space every new thing is made into. The hollow of a mould, the hollow of a hearth, a cupped hand, a womb, the quiet before a word. Before the Sundering it was the Well that let *new* things into the world.
- About 900 years ago **the Crowned**, nine sorcerer-kings and queens, drank from it to make themselves deathless. They drank it dry. A Well drunk dry becomes a hunger, a hollow that wants to be filled. That is what **the Unmaker** was: not a thing from outside but the seventh Well, starved. ("It cannot be killed. It can only be held. And whoever holds it, it will try to become.")
- Aurelian wove the Lattice to put a lid on the starving Well. The same weaving cast the Crowned out beyond the edge of the world: the **Veil** is the seam of their exile, and the Sundered Lands are its scar. The Grey Ferryman rowed them out. The Tithe was fed to the hunger to keep it quiet.
- Book Two ended it: the hunger unfolded into "a small lonely dark", and Malachi (the *seventh* child) sat with it in the deep and sang until he died. With no Tithe and no lid, kindness did what nine centuries of binding could not: **the seventh Well began to heal**. It is young now, enormous and frightened. It is what follows Seren home ("not alone"; "a big shape").
- It learned to speak through Antho during his eight mad years. **The Murmur was its voice.** In Book Three that voice comes back, small and scared, asking for "the lamp-lady" and for "Mal" (the only name it was ever sung). Antho must learn to love the thing that tormented him.
- While it rises, it sheds. Bits of it come up through the ground as **Wild Delves**: unclassified dungeons full of *might-have-been*. Each Delve core is a piece of the Well. Killing a core hurts it, and Antho feels it.
- Attunement stages are the same: Listening → Answering → Speaking → Commanding → Becoming. **Seraphine**, who has never heard any Well, is the first living person to hear this one ("I've always been good at the spaces between letters"). Lex reaches Hollow Listening in Book Three by learning that every casting needs a mould.

### 9.2 The Crowned and the Harrow line
- **The Crowned**: nine sorcerer-monarchs of the Sundering. Four died in the exile. Five survive in the **Outer Court**, a dead grey-gold country beyond the Veil, asleep in silk cocoons, deathless and starving.
  - **Maelis, the Moth Queen** (Book Three villain). Tall, grey-gold, beautiful, eyes like lit windows, wings of silk and dust she wears folded like a cloak. Courteous, patient, funny, *hungry*. She goes towards light: that is what she is. Her servants are the **Mothwing**, people she made deathless by feeding them stolen light: pale, dust-winged, light-eating. Her bite **dims** a person: their warmth goes out and they follow the moth-light. A dimmed person can be **relit** by someone who loves them lighting a lamp and saying their name.
  - **Oszric, the Gilded King**: the eldest. Wakes in the Book Three epilogue. Book Four and Five villain.
  - Three others, still asleep: **Ysenne the Glass Queen**, **Caddoc of the Iron Crown**, and **the Thorn-Crowned** (name unknown).
- **Hesper**, Maelis's younger sister, the tenth heir, refused to drink. She helped Aurelian, gave up her crown, and fled into the world to hide. Her daughters became hedge-witches who never Kindled and never stayed anywhere long, who sang to the dark and *never let a moth in*. The family name was **Harrow** ("to break the ground before sowing"). **Seren Harrow** is the last of that line, so **Lex and Antho are Vey on their father's side and Crowned on their mother's**.
- Harrow blood can open the Veil from inside, the way Vey blood could touch the Root. That is why Maelis wants the brothers and their mother.

### 9.3 Other long threads
- **Arion is ageing fast.** With no Root holding him, two centuries are coming due, roughly a year a week in Book Three. He ends Book Three looking about seventy-five and is happy. He dies in Book Four.
- **Seren gave her memories of Arion to the Ferryman.** She comes home to an old man she does not remember and falls in love with him again. ("You have a nice voice.")
- **Wren is pregnant** (Book Three). A daughter is born early in Book Four. The Gilded King wants the child.
- **Elric** is still in the First Knot, holding the Lockward. The **Key of Shadows** is still barred in the Lock. Maelis's siblings will want it in Book Four: the Veil was woven into the net, and only the Key can unwrite it. Elric speaks once in Book Three.
- **Unbounded growth**: with no Ceiling, the strong grow fast. Book Three's second antagonist, **Silas Hask**, is a Vhessan miner Unbound by Lex who becomes the most powerful Delve-breaker in Caldor and Maelis's champion.
- **Ember** lives in Lex's hearth, writing *The Book of What Was, Volume Two*. Ember is a piece of Arion, and it feels him ageing.

## 11. BOOK FOUR: THE SORCERER'S CROWN (chapter plan)

**Setting:** opens at the end of winter, about three months after Lantern Night (Lark is three months old) and ends on the next Lantern Night, Lark's first birthday, when the Veil falls. ### 11.1 The engine of the book (the Key, the last thread, the crown)
- **The Veil is the last thread of the net.** Aurelian tied it last, into the First Knot, at the bottom of the world, where Elric holds the Key barred with the Lockward. The thread runs from the Knot out to the edge of the world.
- **Caddoc of the Iron Crown is climbing it from the Outer Court**, ringing it with iron as he comes. Where it runs shallow, iron breaks the ground. He will reach the Knot on Lantern Night.
- **The net has had no Root since Lex gave it away.** Whoever turns the Key is offered the whole net: the Root and the crown. A Crowned who turned it would crown the world. Oszric would gild it, with every Ceiling back, forever, and nothing new ever again. ("You left the crown lying in the road, lad.")
- **Only a hand that has already refused a crown can turn the Key and stay itself.** Hesper's crown, the only one of the Ten that never touched the pool, carries that refusal. Whoever wears it says no for them. **Whoever makes the Key's last turn goes with what it unwrites.**
- **Aurelian left the Veil one last turn** (Codex margin, found by Seraphine): *Unwrite it when there is someone on this side who can make room for what is on the other. Not before.* Mal is not ready ("too big... I'm still little"). They will have to do it anyway.
- **Where the crown went:** about thirty-two years ago Wenna Harrow, already dying, took it to the Ferryman. She paid her memory of the man she loved, Seren's father, which is why she never spoke of him. The Ferryman carried it to the far side and set it on the chair in **the tenth tower**, Hesper's empty tower in the Outer Court. Rhosyn found it and grew her thorns round it to hide it from Oszric. Its "no" in the Court kept the cocoons still for thirty years.
- **Crossing:** the Veil opens to Harrow blood. But the grey country pushes anyone with room in them (it would have pulled Hesper apart). The brothers carry **room from Mal**: Ivy's Hush-cup flasks of pool water, where each sip is a day. Hask, who once drank Maelis's light, is barely pushed.

### 11.2 The Crowned in Book Four
- **Oszric, the Gilded King.** GILDING: his touch and his gold dust *keep* things. They turn gold, stopped, unchanging and aware. He drank first because he watched his father waste. He cannot bear an ending. He gilded his whole household at the exile so none would come apart: **the Gilt**, thousands of gold people along the white road, awake for nine hundred years. He wants the smith's child because she is new, the one thing the Crowned can never make (they drank the Well of new things). He wants her as his heir, kept forever. He walks into the world in Part Two. Only an ending frightens him: the Ferryman, who rowed him out ("Not you. You are not on my list. You never will be. That is your punishment, not mine.").
- **Ysenne, the Glass Queen.** She walks in reflections and can look out of any glass, still water or lantern pane, and reach a cold hand through. She is a cold, curious, vain truth-teller who shows people themselves. Her weaknesses are things with no reflection: Wren's mist on glass, Lex's black heat (fire without light), and Mal's pool, which is a hollow and reflects nothing. She runs the Outer Court while Oszric is away.
- **Caddoc of the Iron Crown.** Binding. Grim, honest, joyless and courteous. He keeps his word. "I'm not cruel, smith. I'm only certain." He nodded at Hesper on the shingle. He climbs the last thread and dies with it at the Knot, nodding at the crown: "Hesper's."
- **Rhosyn, the Thorn-Crowned.** Growing things. Her roses became thorns. She listened to Hesper and drank anyway. Gaunt, green-eyed, starving and ashamed. She sends a single rose through the crack ("HES?"), guides the brothers through her thorns to the tenth tower and does **not** betray them, though everyone expects her to. She stays behind holding the thorns and is taken by Ysenne (Book Five).
- **Orrin**, the steward, comes through the crack as dust carrying "a pinch of the King's gold". He offers the dying **"to be kept"**. Those who accept, **the Kept**, become gold in their chairs, eyes moving, unable to speak or die.
- **The Outer Court's dead:** the four cousins who came apart against the edge and everyone who starved since. They drift as grey dust with no river to take them. (Book Five: Mal makes room for them.)

## 12. BOOK FIVE: THE SORCERER'S DAWN (chapter plan — the last book)

**Setting:** opens at Midwinter, about seven weeks after the Veil fell (Lark is a year and two months old). Part One runs through the winter to the spring equinox. Part Two runs to **Midsummer dawn**, the shortest night, when the door is made and the sun rises over the grey country for the first time in nine hundred years. The Epilogue is six years later. Files 00–39: Prologue, Part One (01–19), Interlude (20, unnumbered), Part Two (21–38), Epilogue (39). Printed Part Two chapter numbers run one lower than file numbers.

**Levels.**
- **Lex:** WORLDFORGER Lv 99 all book until Ch 31.
  - Attunements: Tide COMMANDING (Ch 7), Gale COMMANDING (Ch 10), Shade COMMANDING (Ch 12, skipping a stage with Antho beside him), Radiance COMMANDING (Ch 13, learned from Seren: light you give away), Hollow COMMANDING (Ch 23). All seven COMMANDING from Ch 23. Flame offers BECOMING at the forging (Ch 33), and he says no.
  - Ch 31: **Lv 100.** The net offers TIER V — SOVEREIGN and the crown. He refuses. Class: **SMITH**. Grade: **Common**. Ceiling: none.
- **Antho:** THRESHOLD WARDEN Lv 96 → 97 (Ch 12) → 98 (Ch 15). Frozen Ch 19–31. Ch 34: Lv 100. He refuses SOVEREIGN and becomes **DOORKEEPER**, Common.
- **Wren:** MISTWALKER Lv 71 → 72 (Ch 10) → 73 (Ch 18). Frozen. Lv 75 by Ch 37.
- **The freeze (Ch 19–31):** with Ysenne half-seated at the Heart, the net stops all growth. No experience, no level-ups, and Kindlings show the child only their own face. Panels read YOU ARE WHAT YOU ARE. Attunement is UNTRACKED, so the Wells are not frozen, and the door is of the Wells.

### 12.1 The engine of the book
- **The open edge.** With the Veil gone, the world and the grey country simply touch. The grey country is a place with all its room drunk away, and now it drinks the world's room through the open edge. **The greying** creeps east about a mile a day:
  - bread won't rise, seeds won't sprout, eggs won't hatch;
  - cuts close slowly;
  - people feel *stopped*.

  The grey dust drifts east on the wind. Lanterns that are *given* slow it (Ch 13), and the freeze speeds it (Ch 19). By Midsummer it is in the birch wood at Harrow.
- **The dead.** The Outer Court's dead (the four coloured cousins and thousands of grey) drift east to Mal and wait round the rim as columns of dust. **Mal is too little to take them**: "*[...if I let them all in I'll stop...]*". Where they linger the green circle greys.
- **What the Crowned drank is Mal.** When Caddoc fell with the thread, a heavy cold piece of Mal came home and Mal grew (Ch 3). The three living Crowned (Oszric, Ysenne, Rhosyn) still hold most of what was drunk. Mal can grow big enough only if they **pour it back**, as Hesper did at the Drinking. Pouring back ends deathlessness. Lex's rule: *we don't take it. We ask.*
- **A door, not a wall.** Malachi's note in the Codex: *Aurelian built a wall because a wall needs one Well. A door needs all seven, and a smith who can hear every one.* The new edge is **forged, not woven**: of the Wells, not the net. It is a threshold that asks, along the whole length of the edge, taught by one physical door standing on the white road where the Veil hung. Through it lies **Mal's room**: for the dead, the way home; for the grey country, room at last. The door is made of:
  - **leaf:** Old Mag's apple wood (the tree that bloomed in snow);
  - **threshold:** the cracked Ashbourne Lattice Stone that once told Lex he was a Smith;
  - **hinges:** the Key's bow, bit and blade, reforged;
  - **bell:** Malachi's (Hesper's) bell, which rings when someone on the other side is sad;
  - **latch:** **the crown in the road**, unmade by the one who refused it, so it can say both yes and no;
  - **paint:** Harrow blue, mixed by Seren.

  Every Well goes into it: Flame (Lex, Kestrel, the Ashari), Stone, Tide (Teo, the Undertow's quench), Gale (Wren, the First Breath), Shade (Antho: the hinge-dark), Radiance (given lanterns; Seren), and Hollow (Mal, Ivy, Pim, Seraphine: the opening itself).
- **The crown in the road.** The net still has no Root. Its centre, the gold anvil at the Heart (Aurelian's Tomb, across the grey river under the Drowned Country), is empty. **Ysenne** walks into the net through the Lattice Stones, which are glass to her, and goes for the centre. The Root has only ever answered to **Vey blood**, so she needs a Vey hand: Lex, Antho or Lark. Lark is Vey, Harrow and *new*. Half-seated without one (Ch 19), Ysenne can only freeze the net as regent. With Lark's hand she could crown herself: every panel a mirror, every person fixed as what they are now, nothing new ever again.
- **Endings:**
  - **Ysenne** has never seen her own face; mirrors show her everyone but herself, and she wants the net to look at her. At the Heart the net reads her: NOT OF THIS NET — NO RECORD. In Rhosyn's eyes she sees herself at last ("Oh. There I am.") and comes apart into glass dust. Rhosyn carries it to Mal.
  - **Rhosyn** pours back first (Ch 15). She becomes a mortal woman of about forty and plants roses at Harrow.
  - **Oszric** learns from Old Mag's death (Ch 8), from tea with Seren, from Brannoc and from Lark's *No* that an ending can be borne. His keeping is one grip: he cannot let one go without letting all go, himself included (Ch 17). At Midsummer dawn he puts on Hesper's crown, and **its last no** is no to keeping. Everything kept is unkept:
    - Roole, Col Ashby, Tilda's hand, Hask's arm, Wren's fingers, Brannoc's fingertips;
    - the nine thousand, who come apart gently into dust and go home through the door;
    - Ilda, with her mother.

    Oszric pours back and goes through the door holding Rhosyn's hand ("I went in"). The Ferryman: "*Now you're on my list.*"
- **Ember.** In the Ferryman's lantern, without its love for Lex (Interlude). Ch 8: at Old Mag's death it greets Lex politely as a stranger. Ch 29: it rows them to the Heart. Ch 36: the Ferryman, with a door at the edge now, sets his lantern down and gives the flame back to the Harrow hearth. "*A love for a love was the price. The rest isn't mine to keep.*" Ember remembers everything except loving Lex, and has to learn it again, as Seren learned Arion. Ch 38: it calls him *Lex* again.
- **Lark** says *No* to the Root (Ch 30), as she said it to a king.

### 12.2 Chapter plan
- **Prologue: The Glass Queen** (Ysenne). The night the Veil fell. Rhosyn in glass chains. Ysenne looks into every pane, pool and lantern in the world and in none of them is her own face. The crown in the road. "*I'll need a child.*"

**PART ONE: THE OPEN EDGE**
1. **Good Morning** (Lex). Midwinter at Harrow. Grief: the grave by the stream, the TOO LATE lantern, the basket (Key pieces, crown). Panel: Lv 99, and the empty place. Elowen's raven: the Kept are walking into the Westmarch, and at Westerling the bread won't rise. Hook: Ivy's message from Mal: the dead are here and it is too little.
2. **The Bread That Wouldn't Rise** (Lex). Westerling and the old edge with Wren and Elowen. The greying; Seraphine's measure, a mile a day. Nan Ashby and gold Col. The white road and the towers. Fire poured west vanishes like water into sand. Hook: a gold giant came to a farm for tea, asked after everyone's grandmothers and asked the way to Ashbourne.
3. **The Dead at the Rim** (Antho). Mal's pool: Ivy, Pim, Hask, Kit. The dead round the rim, and the four cousins' names. Mal felt Caddoc's piece come home. What the Crowned drank is Mal. Hook: Pim asks the dead for "the bread-woman", and one grey column bends.
4. **Seven Wells** (Lex). Council at the Forge. Malachi's note: a door needs seven. Lex's attunements. The plan in three parts: the door, asking the Crowned, the lanterns. Hook: there's a gold giant on the green.
5. **The King on the Green** (Lex). Oszric beside the cracked Stone. The gold cup. "Cad's gone." Lex asks him to pour back: no, his household would go through the door. Old Mag invites him onto her step. Hook: "Sit with me, lad. When it's time."
6. **Mist on Glass** (Wren). Ysenne in Caldor's mirrors, showing people themselves; the mirror-gazers. Lark's bathwater: "your daughter is a key." Horn panes; Wren's gold fingers and the bow. Hook: Rhosyn's voice in a puddle: Ysy is going into the Stones.
7. **The Undertow** (Lex). With Teo. The vow kept. Tide COMMANDING; the quench that remembers. Hook: the bell — Old Mag.
8. **Ninety-Seven Years** (Lex). Old Mag dies with Oszric holding her hand, not keeping her. The Ferryman, and Ember in the lantern: "*Good evening. Have we met?*" "Still not you." She leaves Lex the apple tree.
9. **Tea for Two** (Seren). A widow's winter. The grey book's last page. Oszric comes back for tea. The forbidden verse of the Lantern Song. Hook: "Ysy asked me to bring her the child. I haven't said no."
10. **The First Breath** (Wren). Wren, Lex and Lark in the eye of the storm. Gale COMMANDING. Hook: every Lattice Stone in Caldor opens an eye.
11. **The Stones Look Back** (Lex). A Kindling that shows a girl only her face. Ysenne in Lex's own panel. Seraphine: she's walking the threads to the Heart, and she needs a Vey hand. Hook: "Your daughter's hands are very square."
12. **The Hinge** (Antho). The brothers in Shade's starlit dark; grief for their father. Shade COMMANDING; Antho Lv 97. Hook: the green circle is greying.
13. **One Thousand One Hundred and Thirteen** (Lex). Seren teaches Radiance: give it away. Every Keep lantern carried west and given; Radiance COMMANDING; the grey slows. Hook: Pim has gone west to keep his promise.
14. **Back Down the White Road** (Pim). Into the open grey country. Ilda, walking. The empty glass palace. Hask's pick finds the fault in Rhosyn's chains. Orrin lets them go. Hook: "Take me to the pool."
15. **The Rose Goes Home** (Seren). Rhosyn pours back at Mal's pool. Roses round the rim. Mal grows and takes in the cousins and hundreds more. Antho Lv 98. Hook: every glass in the Keep cracks: "*Rosie. What have you done?*"
16. **The Apple Tree** (Lex). Felling Mag's tree in blossom. Digging up the cracked Lattice Stone. Seraphine's design for the door. Hook: Oszric: "*Ysy says if I bring her the child the net will keep everyone. No doors at all.*"
17. **What the King Kept** (Oszric). Among his Kept. Roole at the Greyfall gate; he tries to let one go and can't. All or nothing. He says no to Ysenne: his first no.
18. **The Lantern Line** (Wren). The given lanterns along the grey front. Orrin's grey-storm. Ysenne's first reflections step out of the ice; mist undoes them. Wren Lv 73. Hook: every Stone goes dark.
19. **The Empty Chair** (Lex). Ysenne reaches the Heart and sits half-crowned. WORLD NOTICE: A CLAIMANT SITS AT THE HEART. THE NET IS STILL. Hook: "*Bring me the child by Midsummer, or I'll keep the whole world exactly as it is.*"

**Interlude (file 20): The Lantern.** Ember in the Ferryman's lantern, crossing with Arion. Arion on the far shore: "*Look after him. You'll like him. You did before.*" Ember begins Volume Three: *The Book of What Is*.

**PART TWO: THE SORCERER'S DAWN**
21. **Still** (Lex). The world under the freeze. The Wells answer anyway. Midsummer is the deadline. Hook: the latch must say yes and no.
22. **Rosie** (Rhosyn). A mortal at Harrow: bread, rain, roses on Arion's grave. Hook: "*Ysy can't see herself. She never could.*"
23. **The Iron of the Key** (Lex). Hinges from bow, bit and blade. Hollow COMMANDING: all seven. Hook: Oszric at the Forge door.
24. **Brannoc** (Brannoc). The smith and the king: Mattie, outliving people, fingertips. Hook: "*If I let go, I go through that.*"
25. **Ilda** (Pim). Ilda reaches the pool and her mother's dust, and can't go in while she's kept.
26. **The Grey at Harrow** (Seren). The grey in the birch wood. The TOO LATE lantern holds the window.
27. **The Reflections** (Lex). Reflections step out of every glass at the Forge. Lex against himself, crowned. At Harrow, Ysenne's hand comes out of the stream and Lark is gone.
28. **Through the Glass** (Wren). The Mistwalker walks the glass road after her daughter.
29. **The River** (Antho). Antho, Lex and Rhosyn at the grey river. The Ferryman rows them free ("I'd like to see how it ends"). Ember, courteous and curious.
30. **The Heart** (Lex). Ysenne at the gold anvil with Lark. The offer. Wren steps out of the glass. Lark says *No*.
31. **A Hundred** (Lex). Ysenne gives him the hundredth to tempt him. TIER V — SOVEREIGN: refused. SMITH, Common. He takes the crown out of the road and it is metal in his hand. NOT OF THIS NET. "*Oh. There I am.*" WORLD NOTICE: THE NET BELONGS TO NO ONE. The freeze breaks.
32. **Midsummer Eve** (Seren). Everyone goes west. Lanterns along the whole edge. Oszric asks Rosie to walk him to the door.
33. **The Forging of the Door** (Lex). All seven Wells; everyone gives something. Flame offers BECOMING and Lex says no.
34. **The Doorkeeper** (Antho). Lv 100. SOVEREIGN refused: DOORKEEPER. He opens it.
35. **The Last No** (Oszric). Hesper's crown on his head. He opens his hand and everything kept goes free. He pours back and goes through.
36. **Dawn** (Lex). The sun over the grey country. The Ferryman sets his lantern down. Ember comes home. Ilda goes in with her mother. Roole steps out of the gate.
37. **The Book of What Is** (Lex). Summer. The grey goes back and the west greens. Ember learning him again. Antho and Seraphine go to live by the door.
38. **Lantern Night** (Wren). Lark's second birthday. "*Good morning, Lex.*"
- **Epilogue: A Forge, a Child, a Lantern.** Six years on. Lark makes her first lantern and they carry it to the door.

### 12.3 As finished (changes from the plan)
- **Order:** files 2 and 3 swapped (2 is *The Dead at the Rim*, Antho; 3 is *The Bread That Wouldn't Rise*, Lex). Ch 6's hook became the rose by the grave with the silk pricked: "YSY IN STONES / WANTS CROWN IN ROAD / NEEDS VEY HAND / SMALL ONE / HELP ME".
- **Titles as written:** 9 *Tea for Two*; 13 *One Thousand One Hundred and Thirteen*; 18 *The Lantern Line*; 21 *Still*; 32 *The Lantern Road* (not *Midsummer Eve*).
- **Geography:** Harrow is three miles from the Forge, downstream. The grey's leading edge makes a **mirror** wherever it crosses running water (live water backs up against the stillness and goes flat). That is how Ysenne reaches Lark: first up the stream from Seren's pond (Lex snatches Lark back), then through the Ashbourne mill-pond, inside the lantern ring.
- **Ch 26:** Seren carries the light in her own hands (no lantern) to keep the grave; she cuts STILL HERE under TOO LATE.
- **Ch 27:** Reflections come out of the glass panes of the given lanterns. Lex's crowned self draws fire straight through the frozen net. The answer is Brannoc's banked fire: Antho's Shade cups every lantern so nothing can be seen. In the total dark the only light is Lark, who walks to the mill-pond; Ysenne takes her ("That's why I'm not asking"), cuts Rhosyn's hands, and Wren follows as mist. Oszric: "it's time I went and had a word with my sister."
- **Ch 28:** The glass road is where every reflection ever cast is kept. Lark says no to every pane. She turns Ysenne's face and points at the mist: "Mam. There." Wren comes out.
- **Ch 29:** Antho calls the Ferryman at the Forge doors with Wenna's clay lamp. Four cross: Antho, Lex, Rhosyn and Oszric ("not on my list", so there's room). Lex gives Hesper's crown to Oszric. Ember: "Good morning... It just seemed like the right thing to say." The six lanterns in the Drowned Country square are still burning, and the dead there turn east.
- **Ch 30:** YOU ARE WHAT YOU ARE stills everyone but Lark, who has no panel. Mal makes a hair of room in front of Lex, enough to say her name. The net **asks** ("WILL YOU HOLD IT?"); it learned to ask from the last Root who let go. "A ROOT CANNOT BE TAKEN. A ROOT CAN ONLY BE GIVEN." Ysenne gives Lex the last four points.
- **Ch 31:** Wren: "I'd miss trees." SMITH, Common, Lv 100, Ceiling NONE. The crown is beaten into a thumb-latch on the first anvil; Lark strikes the last blow. ITEM: NOT OF THIS NET / NO RECORD. Rhosyn turns Ysenne's face like Lark did: "Oh. There I am." The reflections go free. THE NET BELONGS TO NO ONE.
- **Ch 32–33:** The Ashbourne Stone comes up at Lex's asking: THRESHOLD, 2,881 Kindlings. Seraphine: the edge is a hole the world's room drains through. Nineteen days west on a green road, a lantern left every mile. The six helpers are Teo (Tide), Wren (Gale), Antho (Shade), Seren (Radiance), Brannoc (Stone, standing on it closes the crack) and Mal (Hollow, the room behind the door); Lex is Flame. BECOMING refused. When the latch drops the wind stops and the whole edge draws in to the frame. Everyone paints the door; Lark's blue handprint goes under the latch. Then a knock from the other side.
- **Ch 34:** The latch won't lift for Lex. Antho: Lv 99 → 100, SOVEREIGN ("none pass but by your leave") refused, DOORKEEPER, Common. Behind the door is Mal's room, with the pool and Pim at the bottom of it. The Ferryman knocked; "May I come in?" "Yes. Mind the step." He sits on the threshold in the sun.
- **Ch 35:** The crown's last no opens Oszric's hand for him. Recent kept come back alive (Roole, Col, every gilded finger, Antho's coin); Bet Dale dies to go and see her boy; the old nine thousand go like sand, but the dead come out of the door to each of their own. Ilda: "I'm happy. That's happy." Oszric pours back the first share (endings), shrinks to an old man, and the Ferryman writes him on the list. He walks through with Rosie to the pool, gives Mae's wire crown to the water, and dies holding Pim's hand. Orrin follows.
- **Ch 36:** Colour comes back with the sun. WORLD NOTICE: THE EDGE OF THE WORLD IS A DOOR / "Mind the step." The Ferryman gives Ember back ("A love for a love... The rest isn't mine") and walks east to see mornings. Ilda and Maudie go into the pool together. Mal is whole. Roole walks down the white road, which ends in Mal's room, and knocks.
- **Ch 37–38:** Ember is set in the Harrow hearth and calls Lex "smith". Seren takes dictation for Volume Three; the secret in Harrow blue is a drop of a Harrow woman's tears. Antho and Seraphine build a house by the door; their son ARION ("Ari") is born in August. Pim brings Hes's crown back and Lark puts it on her rag rabbit: "Yes." On the morning after Lantern Night: "You've got ash on your nose... Good morning, Lex."
- **Epilogue:** Six years on. Lark (8) makes a thick-tinned lantern with YES cut in the base for Brannoc (71), who goes to the door to die. The Ferryman comes back to sit with him. Mattie's dust waits a yard inside the door: "There you are." Lark: "I want to make another one."
