# Continuity Ledger

Running record of levels, stats, skills, items and facts established in the text. Update after every chapter.

## Rules of thumb
- Emberwright: +2 SPI, +1 INT per level automatically, +6 free points per level.
- Health = VIT×10, Mana = SPI×10, Stamina = (MIG+VIT)×5.
- Ember Strike base cost 15 mana (−5 with Anvilsong's Ardent rune = 10).
- Authority regenerates about 1/hr; faster near Stones. True Sight = 1.

## Book One

### Prologue
- 15 years before Ch 1. Lex 4, Antho 9. The seal used 97/100 of Arion's Authority. Lex clutched an iron horseshoe nail. Arion taps two fingers on surfaces (Ember's habit later). Seren had flour on her cheek. Lantern in the window.

### Ch 1
- Lex: 19, [Smith] Common Lv 9, cap 24. MIG 16 AGI 9 VIT 14 INT 13 SPI 4 RES 15. Smithing Journeyman 3 (81%), Hammer Use Apprentice 6, Heat Sense Apprentice 2, Endurance Apprentice 4.
- Brannoc: 58, [Smith] Lv 24 for 19 years, bald, soot beard, hums hymns, cooks eggs on the anvil. Cat: Slag (grey tom).
- Villagers: Mother Orla (Crooked Kettle), Pell (miller's son, 15, Kindling in 9 days), Reeve Dunstan, Dunstan twins, Farmer Tobbin, Old Mag (herbwoman, 90, one good eye), Sister Agathe (chapel), Gaffer Wim, the Tanners (sheep taken by Gloam).
- Antho's Kindling: the Stone "screamed" and flashed black. That night the cottage burned. Seren vanished, no body found, iron pots melted. Antho seen walking barefoot into the Greyspine 3 days later. Lex was 11.

### Ch 2
- Elric Thorne: 61, raven Quill (talks a few words), grey cloak with brass lightning charms, ash staff, scar from left temple into beard, storm-grey eyes. Says he was Arion's friend for 40 years. Searching 11 years.
- Blood test at the Stone: gold flare, SPI shows masked value. Elric's envious look.
- Gloamhounds attack: black mist, white eyes, whisper "bright." Cold iron hurts them a little.
- Seal cracked to 58%. Ember online. Class [Emberwright] (Unique, no cap) accepted at Lv 1; SPI unmasked 4 → 22. Lv 4 by the end of the fight.
- Skills: Ember Strike (new), Forge Sense (evolved from Heat Sense), Runic Forging (sealed).

### Ch 3
- Lex named Ember. Ember's memory is locked in partitions. It knows metallurgy and has opinions on tea.
- Old Mag: Seren knew what the boys were.
- Allocation at Lv 4 (18 points): VIT+5, AGI+5, SPI+4, MIG+2, RES+2 → MIG 18 AGI 14 VIT 19 INT 16 SPI 32 RES 17. HP 190, MP 320, ST 185.
- Authority 9/100 (used 1 on True Sight of Elric → 8).
- Elric's True Sight: Stormcaller (Epic), Tier III Lv 67. MIG 12 AGI 21 VIT 18 INT 44 SPI 51 RES 30. Titles: Warden (revoked), Tempest of Varrow Ford. Sanction Tier IV DENIED ×3 ("twice by the Spire, once by someone else").
- Elric's history: Ceiling added by the Wardens about 300 years ago; the Gloam first appeared 16 years ago in Vhessa; Arion investigated it and vanished after.
- Anvilsong forged (horseshoe nail folded into the core). SUPERIOR. Runes: Ardent (+25% Ember Strike, −5 mana), Steadfast (unbreakable while maker lives), Homeward (returns, 30 paces), ??? (unreadable fourth rune, an open hand; not one of Ember's).
- Runic Forging Novice 6. Lex is Lv 5 at the end (SPI 34, INT 17, 6 free points unspent).
- Brannoc gave Lex a heavy purse ("eight years of wages") and a pack. Hammer-oath: Lex will come back.
- Warden riders (a dozen-plus, grey cloaks, silver lattice banner) are seen heading for Ashbourne from the south, too soon to be from Millbridge.
