# Continuity Ledger

Running record of levels, stats, skills, items and facts established in the text. Update after every chapter.

## Rules of thumb
- Emberwright: +2 SPI, +1 INT per level automatically, +6 free points per level.
- Health = VIT×10, Mana = SPI×10, Stamina = (MIG+VIT)×5.
- Ember Strike base cost 15 mana (−5 with Anvilsong's Ardent rune = 10).
- Authority regenerates about 1/hr; faster near Stones. True Sight = 1.

## Book One

### Prologue
- 15 years before Ch 1. Lex 4, Antho 9. The seal used 97/100 of Arion's Authority. Lex clutched an iron horseshoe nail. Arion taps two fingers on surfaces (Ember's habit later). Seren had flour on her cheek. Lantern in the window.

### Ch 2
