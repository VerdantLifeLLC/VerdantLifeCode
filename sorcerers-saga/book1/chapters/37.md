He didn't throw Gloam. He didn't swing a weapon. He didn't send the Quiet. He stood at the edge of the pit with one white hand still pressed flat against the black crystal, the threads still running from it into him, and he *reached*.

Not for their bodies. For their threads.

Not his breath. Something under both. The silver threads that ran out of him into the great net of the Lattice. Every skill. Every level. Every point he'd ever spent. The hand *squeezed*.

Lex staggered. His panel flickered. Ember Strike went grey. Ironskin went grey. Heat Shaping. Slag Shot. Kindle. One after another, his skills dimmed, like lamps being turned down in a house at night.
