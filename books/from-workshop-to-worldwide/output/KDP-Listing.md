## Categories (pick 3 in KDP)

KDP lets you choose up to three categories. Use these, in order of priority:

1. **Business & Money › Small Business & Entrepreneurship › Home-Based Businesses**: closest match to the audience, with less competition than general entrepreneurship.
2. **Business & Money › Small Business & Entrepreneurship › Entrepreneurship**: the broad, high-traffic category for starting a business.
3. **Business & Money › Small Business & Entrepreneurship › Startups**: buyers looking for "how to launch" guides.

Alternates if any of these aren't offered for your format, or if you later rotate categories:
- Business & Money › Small Business & Entrepreneurship › Small Business
- Crafts, Hobbies & Home › Crafts & Hobbies: choose a business- or general-crafts subcategory if the picker offers one. It reaches makers who browse crafting books.
- Business & Money › Marketing & Sales › Marketing › Small Business

Category names differ slightly between paperback and Kindle and change over time. Pick the closest match in KDP's category picker. Before you commit, open each category on Amazon and confirm it has a real bestseller list.

## Extra exposure tips

- **Pricing:** price the Kindle edition between $2.99 and $9.99 to earn the 70% royalty rate. A launch price of $2.99–$4.99 helps early sales and reviews. Many comparable 8.5 × 11 paperback workbooks sell for $14.99–$19.99.
- **Kindle Unlimited:** consider enrolling the eBook in KDP Select. Kindle Unlimited readers find a lot of how-to books.
- **A+ Content:** after publishing, add A+ Content in KDP Marketing. Good images to use: the "sweet spot" diagram, the 30-Day Launch Plan graphic, and a worksheet page. These turn browsers into buyers.
- **Author Central:** claim your author page as The Skill Junky, add the bio and a photo or logo, and link both formats so they show together.
- **Reviews:** give advance copies to friends in maker and small-business groups, then ask them for honest reviews.
- **Your own words:** don't put competitor brand names (such as other marketplaces or authors) in keywords. KDP can reject keywords that are misleading or reference other brands.
